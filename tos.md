# Data Processing Notice for RomAssistant

**Effective date:** 06-08-2026

## Purpose

RomAssistant is an internal Discord automation tool used by the administrators of Discord Community server to assist with reward management and community activities.

RomAssistant is not a user-operated service. Community members do not directly use or control the Bot.

## Data Collection

RomAssistant may process information from Discord channels where it has been configured and authorized to operate, including:

* Discord user IDs
* Discord usernames or display names
* Messages or information submitted in designated channels
* Information related to reward eligibility or participation (screenshot, character id and server)

Certain information processed by RomAssistant may be exported to an external data source accessible only by authorized administrators for reward management purposes.

## Use of Information

Information processed by RomAssistant is used only for:

* Managing community rewards
* Verifying participation requirements
* Sending reward codes or related communications
* Maintaining records needed for community administration

Information collected by RomAssistant is not sold, publicly shared, or used for unrelated purposes.

## Direct Messages

RomAssistant may send Discord direct messages to users when necessary, such as when delivering reward codes.

Users may disable direct messages from RomAssistant, but doing so may prevent delivery of rewards or other related notifications.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for community administration purposes.

This notice may be updated when the functionality of RomAssistant changes.
