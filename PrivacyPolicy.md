# Privacy Policy for RomAssistant

**Effective date:** [Date]

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how RomAssistant ("the Bot") collects, uses, and stores information from Discord users.

RomAssistant is an internal Discord automation tool operated by community administrators. It is used to assist with reward management, participation tracking, and sending reward codes to eligible users.

RomAssistant does not provide a user-facing service and is not operated as a commercial application.

## 2. Information We Collect

RomAssistant may process the following information from Discord:

* Discord user ID
* Discord username and display name
* Messages or information submitted in configured Discord channels
* Information related to participation, eligibility, or reward distribution

RomAssistant only processes information from Discord servers and channels where it has been granted the required permissions.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information processed by RomAssistant is used for:

* Tracking participation in community activities
* Determining eligibility for rewards
* Managing and distributing reward codes
* Sending direct messages containing rewards or related information
* Maintaining administrative records

The information collected is not used for advertising, profiling, selling, or any unrelated purpose.

## 4. Storage and Third-Party Services

Some information processed by RomAssistant may be stored in Google Sheets for administrative purposes.

These spreadsheets are accessible only by authorized administrators responsible for managing the community and rewards.

RomAssistant does not sell, publish, or otherwise distribute personal information to third parties.

## 5. Discord Data

RomAssistant operates through the Discord platform and may access information provided through Discord's API.

Use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

## 6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for community administration, reward tracking, and record-keeping purposes.

When information is no longer needed, it may be deleted or removed from administrative records.

If you would like to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information stored by RomAssistant, you may contact the administrator.

## 8. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to restrict access to information processed by RomAssistant. Access to stored information is limited to authorized administrators.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in RomAssistant's functionality or data handling practices.

Continued participation in the community after changes are made indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
